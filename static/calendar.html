Deadline Families

Cumulative "by date" tranches of the same event. The gap p(far) − p(near) prices the window; the conditional column is P(event in window | not yet). Flagging a window creates a manual signal on the far tranche (long-window trade = buy far-YES + buy near-NO; the near leg is yours to pair). MISMATCH = tranche descriptions differ in resolution-relevant wording — the MicroStrategy May-31 failure mode; treat the pair as non-fungible.
